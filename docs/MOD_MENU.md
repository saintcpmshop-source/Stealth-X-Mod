## 🚀 How to Open the Mod Menu

1. Launch **Stealth X**
2. Look for the **floating purple icon** on the screen
3. Tap it to open the Mod Menu
4. Toggle any feature on/off instantly

> 💡 Changes apply **immediately** — no restart needed.

## 📋 Feature List

### 🚫 1. Remove Ads
Disables all ad banners, popups, and interstitials.

### 🛒 2. Free In-App Purchases
Items can be purchased when the price is displayed as **0.1**.

### ⛽ 3. Unlimited Fuel
Fuel gauge never depletes — drive forever.

### 🛡️ 4. Vehicles Cannot Be Damaged
No crash, collision, or fall damage to your vehicle.

### 🚓 5. Unlock Police Car Purchase Option
Police vehicles become available in the shop.

### 🚗 6. Unlimited Vehicle Purchases
Buy any vehicle without purchase limits.

### 👁️ 7. Unlock Demon Eye (Avoid Goats)
The Demon Eye accessory is unlocked and can avoid goats.

### 💵 8. Unlimited Cash
Instant high cash balance — never run out.

### 🪙 9. Unlimited Coins
Instant high coin balance — never run out.

### ⚙️ 10. Unlock W16 Engine
The top-tier W16 engine is available for all compatible vehicles.

### 📯 11. Unlock Horns
All horn sounds are unlocked.

### 💨 12. Unlock Smoke Colors
All exhaust smoke colors are unlocked.

### 🚀 13. Super Acceleration
Extremely high acceleration. ⚠️ Vehicles may slide easily.

### ❄️ 14. Freeze Vehicles (NPC)
NPC vehicles remain stationary after entering the map.

### 🚦 15. No Traffic Vehicles
No new vehicles will spawn. Existing vehicles will remain.

### 👻 16. Vehicle Penetration
Vehicles can pass through other cars on the road.

### 🗺️ 17. Map Editor Available
Full Map Editor enabled. Map components translated into **Chinese**.

### 💡 18. Unlock Vehicle Lights
All vehicle light styles are unlocked.

### 🏠 19. Unlock House 3
Premium House 3 is unlocked.

### 👑 20. Unlock Premium Membership Features
All premium membership perks are active.
