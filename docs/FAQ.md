## 🔒 Safety & Banning

### Is Stealth X safe?
The mod is designed for offline use. Online play carries a ban risk — always use a throwaway account.

### Will I get banned?
If you play **online with mods active**, yes, there is a risk. Play offline for the safest experience. No anti-ban system is 100% safe.

### Does it need root?
No. Stealth X works on non-rooted devices.

### Does it work on iOS?
No, Android APK only.

---

## 🎮 Mod Menu

### Where is the Mod Menu?
After launch, tap the **floating purple icon** on screen to open the Mod Menu.

### What does "Free In-App Purchases" mean?
Items can be purchased when the price is displayed as **0.1**.

### What is "Super Acceleration"?
A toggle that gives very high acceleration — but vehicles may slide easily.

### What does "Freeze Vehicles" do?
NPC vehicles remain stationary after entering the map.

### What does "No Traffic Vehicles" do?
No new vehicles spawn; existing vehicles remain on the road.

### What is "Vehicle Penetration"?
Vehicles can pass through other cars on the road.

### Is the Map Editor in English?
The Map Editor is available, and its components are translated into **Chinese**.

## 🏎️ MOD Vehicles

### How do I get the MOD vehicles?
The 16 exclusive MOD vehicles are already unlocked and available in the car list (see [FEATURES.md](./FEATURES.md) for car numbers).

### Are the MOD vehicles free?
Yes — all 16 MOD vehicles are unlocked at no cost.

### Can I customize MOD vehicles?
Yes — paint, rims, lights, horns, and smoke colors all work.

## 💰 Money & Economy

### Why does my money reset?
Try restarting the app once. If it persists, toggle "Unlimited Cash" off and on in the Mod Menu.

### Do I need to buy the game?
No — but we **strongly recommend supporting the original developers** by purchasing the official game.

## 📥 Download & Updates

### Where do I get updates?
Watch the repo → [Releases page](https://github.com/saintcpmshop-source/stealth-x-mod/releases).

### Can I update without losing data?
Yes — install the new APK over the top. Don't uninstall first.

## ⚠️ Problems

### The app crashes
Clear cache, restart device, and ensure at least 1 GB free storage.

### Multiplayer won't work
Use offline mode for the safest experience. Online play may be detected.

### Purchases don't work
Ensure the Mod Menu is open and "Free In-App Purchases" is toggled on.
